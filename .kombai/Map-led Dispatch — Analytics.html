TSB model reference

Estimated thermal safety buffer

The displayed buffer estimates remaining time before the modeled quality limit, minus time to reach cooling.

TSB hours = (θ critical − (T initial − T base )) / (k × (T ambient − T base ) × β insulation )
T base · 4°C
k · 0.15
θ critical · 850 degree-hours
β · 0.4–1.0

Model parameters and risk bands are estimates in this MVP, not certified safety limits.
