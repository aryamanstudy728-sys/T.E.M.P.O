Thermal safety buffer (TSB)

Reference formula and current dashboard model inputs.

TSB_hours = (θ_critical − (T_initial − T_base))
÷ (k × (T_ambient − T_base) × β_insulation)
θ_critical 850 degree-hours T_base 4 °C k 0.15 β_insulation 0.4–1.0
Formula coefficients and risk-band thresholds are estimated model inputs. No band guarantees a stop is safe.
